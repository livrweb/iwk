SLIME KNIGHT

a bouncy hero with no hands and no arms, just a suit of armor and momentum. bunny-hop through trap-filled handcrafted levels and see if you can find the 10 Coin Coin.

what it is

no hands. no arms. just a suit of armor that really wants to get to the end of the level. Slime Knight is a bunny-hopping platformer built around momentum — you don't walk so much as you fling yourself forward and hope the landing works out.

the levels are handcrafted and stacked with traps, because a fair level is a boring level. somewhere in there is a 10 Coin Coin. nobody's told you where.

what's in it

  • momentum-based bunny-hop movement — no walk cycle, just bounce
  • handcrafted levels built to punish confidence
  • a suit of armor with no hands or arms, somehow still platforming fine
  • a secret 10 Coin Coin hidden somewhere in the game
